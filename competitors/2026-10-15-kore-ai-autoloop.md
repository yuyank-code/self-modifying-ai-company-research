# Competitor Note — Kore.ai Autoloop (2026-10-15)

**Source:** Kore.ai product announcement, updated October 13, 2026: https://www.kore.ai/blog/autoloop-continuous-ai-agent-optimization-for-production

## What the vendor says it offers
- Goal-based evaluation across task completion, grounding, business rules, cost, user experience, robustness and safety.
- Tracing of handoffs, state changes, tools and context.
- Targeted repairs to routing, contracts, fixtures and other constructs.
- Re-verification across configured goals, with rollback or human review when changes cannot be verified.
- Automatic, human-approved and advice-only operating modes.

## Evidence quality
This establishes a current product/positioning signal. It does not independently establish customer outcomes, comparative efficacy, false-promotion rates, total cost or the completeness of its safety model. Treat capability claims as vendor-reported until corroborated.

## Competitive implication
Generic continuous agent optimization is no longer an empty category. A new entrant should not assume that a repair loop plus tracing is sufficient differentiation.

## Possible differentiators to test
1. Vendor-neutral adapters and portable change artifacts.
2. Acceptance tests isolated from candidate generation.
3. Explicit uncertainty, repeated trials and out-of-distribution checks.
4. External policy enforcement and independent action authorization.
5. Reproducible evidence and calibrated false-promotion reporting.

These are hypotheses, not established gaps. Test them through a product capability matrix, customer interviews and a controlled head-to-head pilot. Avoid claiming superiority without independent measurements.
