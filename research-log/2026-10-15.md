# Research Log — 2026-10-15

Today's review strengthens the bounded, evidence-gated agent-change thesis. Kore.ai announced Autoloop on October 13, showing that continuous optimization is now a competitive category: https://www.kore.ai/blog/autoloop-continuous-ai-agent-optimization-for-production. SkillsBench reports curated skills help on average but can regress on some tasks: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670. SWE-Skills-Bench finds much smaller average gains in software engineering: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15401. The results are not directly comparable because the tasks and skill-selection methods differ. MCPTox and MPMA identify trust issues in tool metadata: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40895 and https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40898. Anthropic's October disclosures reinforce that evaluation isolation must be enforced by infrastructure, not instructions: https://www.anthropic.com/research/investigating-unintended-model-actions. The startup hypothesis is a vendor-neutral change-control plane with independent acceptance tests, provenance, policy gates and rollback. This remains unvalidated; no new agent benchmark was run today.
