# Deep Research Log — 2026-10-13

Today's pass investigated harness evolution, sequential acceptance, checkpoint retention, and the competitive boundary for an adaptive-software assurance startup. The strongest new benchmark is EvoHarnessBench (arXiv:2609.04280): 17 harness streams, 802 tasks, 520 tools, 42 skills, and 62 agents; reported performance drops as catalogs expand, with retention and adaptation sometimes in conflict. PACE (arXiv:2606.08106) makes the acceptor/commit rule a first-class statistical problem.

E182 ran 5,000 synthetic lineages × 8 checkpoints. History search with point gates recovered more useful checkpoints but increased unsafe acceptance (4.29%); history search with multiplicity-adjusted lower confidence bounds observed no unsafe promotions and recovered 246.6 useful promotions/1,000 lineages versus 156.0 for latest-only adjusted gates. These are synthetic results only, not real-agent evidence.

Competitive conclusion: GitLab's Governed Software Factory, Arize AX/Phoenix, and Kore.ai Autoloop make generic agent assurance/optimization a crowded wedge. Test a narrower cross-vendor harness-evolution compatibility and capability-retention gate.
